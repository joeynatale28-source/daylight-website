Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Daylight is a web calendar for events and tasks. This policy explains, in plain language, the information Daylight handles when you use the service.

Information you provide

Daylight may handle account information, calendar events, tasks, sharing details, and information you choose to provide while using the app.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the calendar, tasks, sharing, authentication, security, and support features you request. Daylight does not sell your personal information.

Sharing and AI access

When you share a calendar or event, Daylight uses the permissions you select. If you connect ChatGPT or Claude, access is scoped to the permissions you grant and can be revoked. Daylight records AI actions in an activity log so you can review them.

Data retention and security

Daylight keeps information for as long as needed to provide the service or as required for legitimate operational and legal purposes. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Daylight changes. The date above shows when the latest version was published.

Questions

If you have a privacy question, use the support channel provided inside Daylight.
