Questions

Good to know.

Is Daylight free?+

Yes. Daylight is free, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. Daylight runs in your browser.

Can I import my calendars?+

Yes. Subscribe by iCal link or import an ICS file. A US Holidays calendar is built in.

Can I share with people?+

Yes. Share calendars or single events with other Daylight accounts, or create a public link.

Does it work with AI assistants?+

Yes. Connect ChatGPT or Claude with scoped, revocable permissions and a full activity log.
