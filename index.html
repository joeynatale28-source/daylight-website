Sharing

Share a calendar, not a screenshot.

Invite people by name and choose whether they can view or edit. Share a whole calendar or a single event with other Daylight accounts, or publish a public link for everyone else. Change your mind whenever you like: access can be revoked at any time.

  • View-only or can-edit, per person
  • Share a whole calendar or a single event
  • Public links for anyone, revoked anytime

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Daylight is free, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Daylight runs in your browser, so there is nothing to download or install.

Can I import my calendars?

Yes. Subscribe by iCal link or import an ICS file. A US Holidays calendar is built in.

Can I share with people?

Yes. Share calendars or single events with other Daylight accounts, as view-only or can-edit, or share with a public link.

Does it work with AI assistants?

Yes. Connect ChatGPT or Claude with scoped, revocable permissions, and review a full activity log of what they do.

Who made it?

An independent developer. Daylight is designed and built by Joey.
