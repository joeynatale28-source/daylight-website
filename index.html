1.0 Sharing →
  1. 1.1

    Invite Daylight accounts

    Share a whole calendar or a single event with another Daylight account. They see it in their own Daylight, without a screenshot or an exported file.

  2. 1.2

    View-only or can-edit

    Decide whether each person can only look or can also make changes. You set the level when you share, so nobody gets more access than you meant to give.

  3. 1.3

    Public links

    Create a public link for people who don’t have a Daylight account. It’s a simple way to put a schedule in front of anyone.

  4. 1.4

    Your account stays yours

    Sharing a calendar shares that calendar, not everything else. Your other calendars and tasks aren’t part of what you hand over.

Let ChatGPT or Claude into your calendar. On your terms.

Connect an AI assistant to Daylight with scoped, revocable permissions. Every action it takes lands in a full activity log you can read.

2.0 AI access →
  1. 2.1

    ChatGPT or Claude

    Connect ChatGPT or Claude to your Daylight calendar. Your assistant can then work with your events and tasks instead of guessing at them.

  2. 2.2

    Scoped permissions

    Decide what an assistant is allowed to do before it connects. Access stops at what you granted, and nothing more.

  3. 2.3

    Revoke any time

    Disconnect an assistant whenever you like from Settings. Once you revoke it, its access to your calendar ends.

  4. 2.4

    A full activity log

    Every action an assistant takes is recorded in an activity log. Open Settings, then AI access, to see exactly what it did and when.
