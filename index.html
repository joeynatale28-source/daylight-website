AI access

Your AI can use your calendar

Ask Claude what’s on your day, or tell it to add an event.

Same for ChatGPT — your calendar, in conversation.

Gemini connects the same way.

  1. 1Connect — link Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini over MCP from Settings → AI access.
  2. 2Choose what it can do — read, write, or delete. Your call, changeable anytime.
  3. 3Just ask — what’s on my day, move lunch to 1, add a dentist appointment.

Daylight connects to your AI over MCP (Model Context Protocol) — one standard link for Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. Every action shows up in Recent AI activity. Disconnect anytime.
