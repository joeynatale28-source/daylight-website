Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Daylight runs in your browser.

Can I import my calendars?

Yes. Subscribe with an iCal link or add an ICS file.

Can I share with people?

Yes. Share with other Daylight accounts as view-only or can-edit, or publish a public link.

Does it work with AI assistants?

Yes. You can connect ChatGPT or Claude with scoped permissions, and revoke access whenever you want.

Who made it?

An independent developer. Daylight is designed and built by Joey.
