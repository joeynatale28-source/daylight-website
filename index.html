Share a calendar, not a screenshot.

Invite people by name and choose whether they can view or edit. Share a whole calendar or a single event with other Daylight accounts, or publish a public link. Change your mind whenever you like.

1.0 Sharing →
  1. 1.1
    Invite by name

    Share a whole calendar or a single event with other Daylight accounts. Pick the people you want by name.

  2. 1.2
    View-only or can-edit

    Choose what each person is allowed to do. Someone who only needs to look can stay view-only.

  3. 1.3
    Revoke anytime

    Access is yours to take back whenever you like. Nothing is permanent unless you want it to be.

  4. 1.4
    Public links

    Publish a public link to share a calendar with anyone. You can revoke it whenever you want.

Let ChatGPT or Claude into your calendar. On your terms.

Connect an assistant to your calendar with scoped permissions that you control. Every connection can be revoked in one click, and a full activity log shows what each assistant has done.

2.0 AI access →
  1. 2.1
    Connect ChatGPT or Claude

    Link an assistant to your calendar from Settings. Daylight works with both ChatGPT and Claude.

  2. 2.2
    Choose read, write, or delete

    Give each assistant scoped permissions instead of full access. You decide what it is allowed to do.

  3. 2.3
    Revoke anytime

    Every connection is revocable. Cut off an assistant whenever you want and its access ends.

  4. 2.4
    Full activity log

    Review what every assistant has done in one place. You always know what was read, added or changed.
