A few good questions

Good to know.

What is daylight?

daylight is a personal calendar app designed to bring your calendars, events, and tasks together in a clean, focused space.

Is it free?

Yes. daylight is free to use.

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by the owner. daylight is a personal project and does not have public sign-up.

Does it work with my existing calendars?

You can subscribe to calendars and import or export .ics files. Google calendars can be subscribed to as well.

Who made it?

Joey, a student developer.
